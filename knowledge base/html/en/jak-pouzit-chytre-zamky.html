Prepare a smart lock for reservations

If TTLock access is enabled for your account, connect the lock to the property and verify the PIN and its validity before sharing it.

Check availability first

Open Smart locks or the property's locks. If the option is absent, ask support about availability. Remote PIN management requires a compatible online gateway that can reach the lock; a phone does not replace it.

Follow the connection wizard

Enable TTLock integration and share the lock with the integration account using the details shown in the app. Load locks and assign the correct lock to the property. Enable automatic PINs after checking connectivity.

Verify before arrival

Check the reservation's PIN status and validity window; PIN history helps trace changes. Resolve errors or unclear results before sending the code. Recheck access after stay changes. Keep a backup key handover procedure.
