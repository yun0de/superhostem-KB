Edit your profile in settings

Check personal account details and language. Manage property and billing details in their respective sections.

Help centreLibrary

Settings → Profile

Open Settings and Profile. Edit the available name, phone and language fields and save. Verify the saved value. If email is not editable, use account management or contact support.

Your profile is not operational settings

Editing a personal profile does not itself change property addresses, municipality accounts, Ubyport or details on every issued document. Check those in the relevant feature settings. If using organisations, confirm the active one.
