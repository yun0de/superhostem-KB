Start with one real stay

Create a property, connect a reservation source or add a direct stay. Send the form to all guests and check their details. The same stay supports records, Ubyport, fees, invoices and messages.

Add features as needed

A guest guide provides arrival instructions. Cleaners get their own access. The public website can accept direct inquiries. Advanced features such as smart locks or online payments depend on account access and setup.

Verify the result

An imported stay is not a complete register. A completed form is not a police receipt. An invoice is not proof of payment. The guides therefore include checks for the actual outcome.
