Keep guest and stay records in Superhostem

Collect details through the stay's form, check every guest and prepare records for the selected period.

1. Send the guest form

Check dates and guest count in the reservation. Send the available registration link. You can start manually; automation is not required.

Practical online check-in guide

2. Check every guest

Under Registered guests, verify all people and correct errors. The guest guide provides stay instructions; it is not the registration form or a replacement for records.

3. Prepare records for a period

Open Guest register and select the property. With one property you may be taken directly to its records. Choose from/to dates and prepare the available PDF. Check missing people and incorrect details before archiving or printing.

4. Check follow-up duties

A completed form does not confirm police reporting or fee payment. Verify Ubyport results and municipality documents separately. See the related legal guide for recordkeeping and retention requirements.
