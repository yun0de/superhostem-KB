Add reservations to a calendar app

Subscribe in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or another iCal-compatible app. A one-off file import does not update itself.

Copy the Superhostem export first

In iCal export, select properties and visible details, then copy the generated URL. The link can expose personal details; share only what is needed. Check missing data in the reservation itself.

iPhone: add a calendar subscription

In Calendar, open Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar. Enter the URL and follow the prompts; in iOS 26 find the subscription, name it and confirm. Labels may vary by version.

Current Apple instructions

Google Calendar and Android

On a computer, open Google Calendar. Next to Other calendars, choose + → From URL and paste the export link. Use the same Google account on your phone and check calendar visibility. Add a new subscription through the website, not the mobile app.

Updates and alerts

The calendar app controls refresh timing and notification options. Check its settings; changes may not appear immediately. For team use and easy revocation, use a separate web link instead. The export is an overview; edit reservations in their source or relevant detail.
