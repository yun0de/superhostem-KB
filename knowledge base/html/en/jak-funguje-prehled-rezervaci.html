Practical guides

Work with the reservations overview

Find stays that need attention, then open their details to handle guests, messages or reporting.

Help centreLibrary

Choose dates and property

Open Reservations. Check past/upcoming dates, status and property filters. Use the list to find a stay or the calendar to compare dates.

Daily check

  1. Review upcoming arrivals.
  2. Open stays missing details or confirmation.
  3. Check guest count and completed forms.
  4. For foreign guests, also verify the Ubyport result.

Reservation status, completed guest details and accepted reports are different information.

Missing or incorrect data?

Clear filters and check the source first. Sync can refresh imports but cannot supply data absent from the source. Use New reservation for a direct stay after ruling out duplicates.
