Practical guides

What to do in reservation details

Open one stay and choose your task: check details, send a form, verify a message or inspect a report.

Help centreLibrary

Check the guest, dates and source

Check property, arrival, departure, guest count, contact and price. For imports, check the original source and available iCal status. Sync cannot add prices from a source that does not supply them.

Send a form and correct guest details

Distinguish reservation confirmation from the registration form. Check each person under Registered guests. Manually marking a status complete does not prove all guest details were supplied.

Send the correct link step by step

Check communication

A template preview shows rendered content, not actual sending. Verify recipient, time and result in Emails and automation history. Edit property templates at the property and one-off stay text using its available action.

Verify Ubyport

Check foreign guest details, submission and the latest report. Reservation status does not replace individual guest results. Open error details before retrying.

Issue a document and inspect changes

Prepare an invoice from the stay and check its amount and customer. History and diagnostics depend on account permissions; advanced import screens may not be customer-accessible.
